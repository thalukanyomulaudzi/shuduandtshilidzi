A story that dates back to 2021, on a random winter Sunday, when Shudu offered Chichi a lift to church, kicked off a friendship that turned into something that neither of us had expected.

A friendship founded on genuine care for each other, unwavering support through life's seasons, and a whole lot of goofing around. Little did we know that God was cooking our marriage—yes, our marriage!
